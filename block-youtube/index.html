Block YouTube — or just the Shorts

Keep YouTube for lectures and tutorials, lose the endless scroll. Hushblock can block all of YouTube, only YouTube Shorts, or give you a daily allowance.

How it works

Install Hushblock

The Windows or Mac app blocks YouTube in every browser. The extension works in Chrome, Edge and Firefox.

Add youtube.com — or youtube.com/shorts

Block the whole site, or only Shorts so the rest of YouTube keeps working.

Choose when

During a focus session, on a schedule (e.g. weekdays 9–5), or after a daily limit like 20 minutes.

Tips that actually stick

  • Use a daily limit instead of a full block if you need YouTube for work — it resets at midnight.
  • Turn on strict mode (Pro) during exams so you can't unblock early.
  • Block the YouTube app too on Mac and Windows, not just the website.

Questions

Can I block YouTube Shorts but not YouTube?

Yes. Add youtube.com/shorts to your Hushblock block list. Regular videos keep working.

How do I block YouTube on my computer for a set time?

Start a focus session in Hushblock or set a schedule. YouTube stays blocked until the session or schedule ends.

Does it work in Safari?

Yes, the Mac app blocks websites in every browser, including Safari.
