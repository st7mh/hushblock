Why people switch from Cold Turkey

  • App blocking without paying first. Hushblock's free plan blocks up to 3 apps and 5 websites, so you can stop Steam, Discord or a game today and upgrade only if you need more.
  • A focus timer that is the main feature, not an add-on. Start a 25, 50 or 90-minute session in one click and your list is blocked until it ends.
  • Learning goals. Tell Hushblock what you want to learn. It makes a short study plan and blocks everything else; pass a 6-question quiz and the session ends early.
  • Works where Cold Turkey doesn't. The browser extension runs on Linux and Chromebooks too.

When Cold Turkey is the better choice

  • If you prefer to pay once instead of a subscription, Cold Turkey's lifetime Pro license may suit you better.
  • If you need to lock the entire computer for a set time, Cold Turkey has a dedicated mode for that.

Questions

Is Hushblock a good free alternative to Cold Turkey?

Yes. Hushblock blocks websites and desktop apps on Windows and Mac. The free plan includes 5 websites, 3 apps, unlimited focus sessions and one schedule. Strict mode and unlimited lists are part of Pro.

Can Hushblock block apps like Cold Turkey does?

Yes. The Windows and Mac apps close blocked programs — games, Steam, Discord, Telegram, launchers — the moment they open, and block websites in every browser, including Safari.

Can I make Hushblock impossible to turn off?

With Pro, strict mode locks a session: no stopping early, no pausing and no unblocking until the timer ends. You can also protect settings with a password.
