Why people pick Hushblock over Freedom

  • It's actually free to start. No trial countdown: the essentials stay free forever.
  • Daily limits. Allow 20 minutes of YouTube a day; when it's used up, it's blocked until tomorrow.
  • Focus timer + study quizzes. Sessions end when the timer runs out — or when you prove you learned what you planned.
  • Private. Lists and stats stay on your device. No ads, no tracking.

When Freedom is the better choice

  • If you need the same blocks on your phone, Freedom supports iOS and Android; Hushblock is desktop and browser only for now.
