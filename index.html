Questions

Is Hushblock really free?

Yes. Blocking up to 5 sites, unlimited focus sessions, one schedule, one daily limit and a week of stats are free forever. Pro unlocks strict mode, unlimited blocking and more.

Does Hushblock see or sell my browsing data?

No. Your block list, browsing time and statistics are stored only in your browser. Hushblock has no servers that receive this data, and there are no ads or analytics. Read the privacy policy.

Can I block only YouTube Shorts?

Yes. Add youtube.com/shorts to your block list — the rest of YouTube keeps working.

What happens when I try to open a blocked site?

You’ll see a calm block page with a short motivational line and your progress for today. If you allow it, you can get in for a few minutes after a short breathing pause — unless strict mode is on.

Can I stop myself from turning it off?

With Pro, strict mode locks your session: no stopping early, no pausing, no unblocking. You can also protect your settings with a password — ask a friend to set it.

How do I cancel Pro?

Open the extension’s Settings → Your plan → Manage subscription, or go straight to the customer portal and sign in with the email you used to pay. You keep Pro until the end of the period you paid for.

I bought Pro — how do I activate it?

If Hushblock is installed in the browser you paid in, Pro switches on by itself. Otherwise open Hushblock → Hushblock Pro, paste the license key from your receipt email and click Activate. One key works in up to 3 browsers.

Does it work in Edge, Brave or Arc?

Yes — any Chromium-based browser that can install extensions from the Chrome Web Store.
