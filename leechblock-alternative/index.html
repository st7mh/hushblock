Why people move from LeechBlock to Hushblock

  • Distractions aren't only in the browser. Steam, Discord, Telegram and games get closed the moment they open.
  • Less configuration. Choose your sites, press Start — no block sets or time-window syntax to learn.
  • Insights. Focus minutes, streaks and time per website show whether it's working.

When LeechBlock is the better choice

  • If you want something fully free and open source and you love fine-grained rules, LeechBlock NG is excellent.
