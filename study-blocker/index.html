Questions

How do I block distractions while studying?

Install Hushblock on Windows or Mac, pick the apps and sites that distract you, and start a learning goal or focus session. Everything on your list stays blocked until the timer ends or you pass the quiz.

Is there an app that makes you answer questions before unlocking?

Yes. Hushblock's learning goals quiz you on what you planned to study; passing with 80% ends the session early.

Is it free?

Yes. The free plan includes 3 learning goals a day, unlimited focus sessions, 5 blocked sites and 3 blocked apps.
